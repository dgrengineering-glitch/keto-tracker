How this is calculated

Lean body mass (LBM) = weight × (1 − body fat %). If you haven’t entered body fat, LBM is estimated from height and weight (Boer formula) and marked “estimated”.

Maintenance calories (TDEE) use the Katch-McArdle equation as the primary method: resting energy (BMR) = 370 + 21.6 × LBM (kg), multiplied by your activity factor (1.2–1.9). Because it is based on lean mass, it tends to suit people who know their body fat. Mifflin-St Jeor (based on weight, height, age and sex) is shown alongside for comparison. Both are estimates – real needs vary by a few hundred kcal, so adjust based on your actual trend after 2–3 weeks.

Rate of loss: a loss of roughly 0.5–1.0% of body weight per week is widely recommended to help retain lean mass; the default is 0.7%/week. The plan slows to at most 0.5%/week once you are lean (under about 15% body fat for men, 25% for women), because leaner people lose more muscle with aggressive deficits.

Energy maths: about 7,700 kcal per kg of body-weight change. The projection assumes about 85% of the weight you lose is fat and 15% lean tissue – roughly what’s typical when protein is adequate and you do some resistance training. Expect a quicker drop in the first week or two on keto as stored glycogen and water are lost; the trend then settles. For gaining, a 5–10% surplus is used and gain is assumed to be roughly half lean, half fat.

Safety limits: daily calories never go below your estimated BMR, never more than 25% below TDEE, and never below 1,500 kcal (men) or 1,200 kcal (women). Maintenance is recalculated each week as your projected weight falls, so the calorie target steps down gradually.

Protein: around 1.6–2.2 g per kg of lean body mass per day is commonly recommended to preserve muscle while dieting. The planner flags it if your protein % at the plan’s calorie level falls below 1.6 g/kg LBM.

These are general estimates, not medical advice. Speak to your GP before large dietary changes, especially if you take medication for diabetes or blood pressure.

Daily targets

Grams = calories × % ÷ 9 for fat, ÷ 4 for protein and net carbs. Changes save automatically.

AI estimates (xAI Grok)

“Photo (AI estimate)” and “Speak your food” use xAI’s Grok with your own xAI API key. Get one at console.x.ai › API Keys – it’s pay as you go: buy a little prepaid credit under Billing and each photo or meal costs a fraction of a penny.

The key is stored only on this phone (never in backups, never shown in full) and is sent only to xAI (api.x.ai). Leave the model blank for the default (grok-4.3, which reads photos and text). Voice recordings are transcribed with xAI’s speech-to-text (grok-voice-transcribe-2.0).

Backup & restore

Your food log is stored only on this device, inside Safari. Clearing Safari history/website data (or deleting the app) wipes it unless you have a backup. Export a backup regularly and save it to Files or iCloud Drive.

About

Product data from Open Food Facts (free, community-maintained, ODbL licence) – always check against the pack if something looks wrong.

UK/EU labels already list carbohydrate excluding fibre, so by default carbs are treated as net carbs and fibre is shown separately. For US-style labels, switch off “Carbs on label already exclude fibre” for that food and fibre will be subtracted.
